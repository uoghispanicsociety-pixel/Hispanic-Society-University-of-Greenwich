About us

Rooted in culture. Connected by community.

We exist to celebrate Latino culture while creating friendship, belonging and cultural exchange.

Culture

Celebrating the diversity, creativity, traditions and contemporary influence of Latino communities.

Connection

Bringing people together through events, networking, collaborations and shared experiences.

Belonging

A welcoming space to express yourself, build friendships and feel part of something bigger.

Our ambition

Beyond one campus

Greenwich is where we begin. We’re building bridges with other universities, cultural organisations and creative communities across London, so Latino culture has a connected, visible home in the city.

Expect inter-university collaborations, creative partnerships, networking and community initiatives, open to everyone who shares the spirit.
